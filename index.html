How long does it take?+

The timeline depends on your website’s scope and the content available. We’ll discuss the schedule with you before work begins.

How much does a website cost?+

Plans start at ₹15,000. The final price depends on what you need — message us on WhatsApp for a free, no-pressure quote.

Do I own my website?+

Our terms state that the website and its content belong to you once the project is fully paid. Domain, hosting and other third-party services have their own terms.

What services do you offer?+

We offer Website Development and Website Maintenance for businesses across West Bengal. The Growth plan includes monthly website updates and edits.

How do I pay?+

Simple UPI or bank transfer. Usually a part advance to start, and the rest on delivery once you're happy.

What if I need changes later?+

You can request edits anytime. The Growth plan includes monthly updates, or we can handle one-off changes whenever you need them.
